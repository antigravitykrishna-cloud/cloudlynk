Hosted URL: https://streamly-legal.vercel.app/privacy-policy
`app.json` `extra.PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` must point to the live version of this page.

**This is an outline, not the final policy.** It maps every section that the live page should contain, with the v0.7.0 source-of-truth references in this repo. The live page (the legal source of truth) should be written by a lawyer or a privacy-policy template service; this outline exists to make sure no section gets forgotten and to keep the live page in sync with the code.

The policy needs to be consistent with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)** and the **Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures) Rules, 2011**. If you ship to users in the EU/UK, additional GDPR/UK-GDPR clauses are needed — note that in the "Jurisdiction" section as a v0.7.1 task.

## 3. What data we collect

For each item: what, why, where it's stored, how long.

### 3.1 Account data
- **Email address** — for login. Stored in `auth.users` (Supabase). Retained until you delete your account.
- **Display name** — for your channel identity. Stored in `profiles.display_name`. Retained until you delete your account.
- **User ID (UUID)** — internal identifier, generated by Supabase Auth. Retained until you delete your account.
- **Account creation date** — implicit (`auth.users.created_at`). Retained until you delete your account.

### 3.2 Content you upload
- **Videos** — uploaded through the in-app picker, stored in Cloudflare Stream. We hold the metadata (UID, duration, status, thumbnail URL) in our database. Retained until you delete the video or your account.
- **Thumbnails and channel art** — stored in the `channel-media` Supabase Storage bucket. Retained until you delete them or your account.
- **Subtitles (v0.7.0 schema; UI in v0.7.1)** — stored in the `subtitles` table. Retained until you delete them or your account.

### 3.3 Watch activity
- **Watch history** — what you watched, how far you got, whether you finished. Stored in `watch_history`. Retained until you delete your account.
- **Player preferences** — your saved quality and playback speed. Stored in `user_preferences` (new in v0.7.0). Retained until you delete your account.

### 3.4 Payment data (if you subscribe)
- **UPI transaction reference** (the 12-digit TRX ID shown on your UPI app) — stored in `subscription_requests.upi_transaction_id`. Retained for 7 years for tax/audit purposes (India's tax retention rules).
- **Payment screenshot** — the image of your UPI app's confirmation screen. Stored in the `payment-screenshots` Supabase Storage bucket, in a folder named after your user ID. Reviewed manually by our team. Retained for 7 years.
- **Plan and amount** — stored in `subscription_requests`. Retained for 7 years.
- **Plan status on your profile** — `profiles.plan_status`, `plan_started_at`, `plan_expires_at`. Retained until you delete your account.

> **Why 7 years for payment data?** India's Income Tax Act and the 2021 CBDT notification on record-keeping require financial transaction records to be retained for 7 years from the end of the financial year in which the transaction occurred. The rest of your data (videos, watch history, etc.) is deleted when you delete your account, but payment records cannot be deleted on request during this retention window — disclose this clearly.

### 3.5 Device permissions we ask for

- **Camera (`CAMERA`, `RECORD_AUDIO`)** — only when you tap the record button inside the app. Used to record video for your channel. We do not record in the background.
- **Photo library (`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED`)** — only when you tap "select from gallery". Used to pick a video to upload.
- **Storage (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)** — for compatibility with older Android versions. On Android 13+ these are ignored in favor of the scoped media permissions above. On Android 12 and below they let you pick from the device's storage.
- **Internet (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)** — required to upload, play, and sync. Always on for the app to function.

We do **not** ask for: location, contacts, calendar, microphone outside of recording, phone state, call log, SMS, or any other Android permission not listed above.

### 3.6 Data we do NOT collect

To be explicit (DPDPA encourages this):
- We do not collect your name (legal first/last), phone number, or physical address.
- We do not collect your bank account, debit card, or credit card number.
- We do not collect your precise location, contacts list, or calendar.
- We do not collect biometric data.
- We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or crash reporting services.

## 4. Why we collect it (the "purposes")

Per the DPDPA, each purpose must be specific and listed. For Streamly:

1. **To provide the service** — auth, content hosting, playback, cross-device sync of watch history and player preferences.
2. **To process subscription payments** — UPI verification, fraud prevention, retention for tax compliance.
3. **To respond to support requests** — when you email `support@streamly.in`, we use your email and account data to answer.
4. **To enforce our Terms of Service** — investigating abuse, removing content that violates the ToS, banning accounts that violate the ToS.
5. **To comply with Indian law** — tax retention, responding to lawful government requests.

We do **not** use your data for:
- Marketing emails (no marketing at all in v0.7.0)
- Personalized advertising (no ads in v0.7.0)
- Sale to data brokers (no sharing of any kind)
- Training AI models on your content (no AI/ML in v0.7.0)

We will disclose data to Indian government authorities when required by a valid legal process under Indian law (e.g. a court order under Section 91 CrPC, or a direction from CERT-In under the IT Act). We will notify the affected user before disclosure unless we are legally prohibited from doing so.

## 6. How long we keep your data

| Data category | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Account data (email, display name) | Until you delete your account |
| Content you upload (videos, thumbnails) | Until you delete them, or until you delete your account (cascade) |
| Watch history, player preferences | Until you delete your account |
| Payment data (UPI TRX ID, screenshot, plan) | 7 years from the date of the transaction (India tax law) |
| Server logs (Supabase / Cloudflare) | Per the provider's default retention (Supabase: 7 days for function logs; Cloudflare: 30 days for access logs) |
| Backups of the database | 30 days rolling (deleted automatically) |

## 7. How we protect your data

- **Encryption in transit:** all data between your device and our backend is sent over HTTPS. There is no HTTP fallback.
- **Encryption at rest:** Supabase Postgres and Cloudflare Stream both encrypt data at rest by default (provider-managed AES-256).
- **Access control:** only the account owner can read their own data (enforced by Supabase Row Level Security policies on every table). Admins can read payment screenshots and subscription requests to verify payments; no other administrative read paths exist. See `docs/security-audit-v0.7.0.md` for the full RLS audit.
- **No public storage:** the `payment-screenshots` bucket is private. The `channel-media` bucket is public so channel art and thumbnails can be served to viewers, but it only contains files the creator explicitly published.
- **Auth tokens:** your login session is stored on your device. On iOS, the session is stored in the iOS Keychain via `expo-secure-store`. On Android, the session is currently stored in `AsyncStorage` (a known v0.7.0 limitation; v0.7.1 will switch to Android Keystore-backed storage). If your device is lost or stolen while signed in, sign out from another device or contact `support@streamly.in` to terminate the session.
- **Operational security:** we use Supabase's hosted offering; our team accesses the database only through the Supabase dashboard with SSO + 2FA. We do not have direct database access from developer laptops.

## 8. Your rights

Under the DPDPA, you have the right to:

1. **Access** — request a copy of all data we hold about you. We will provide it in a machine-readable format (JSON) within 30 days.
2. **Correction** — correct inaccurate data. The in-app profile editor covers most of this; for anything else, email `support@streamly.in`.
3. **Erasure** — delete your account and all associated data, except for payment records that we are legally required to retain (see §6). The in-app "Delete account" flow does this.
4. **Grievance redressal** — contact our Grievance Officer (see §13).
5. **Nomination** — nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, email `support@streamly.in` with the subject line "Privacy request". We respond within 30 days.

## 9. Children's privacy

Streamly is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. The signup form requires a birth year and rejects users under 18, but this is self-attested and not verified. If you believe a child under 18 has created an account, email `support@streamly.in` and we will delete the account.

## 10. International data transfers

Streamly is operated from India. Your data is primarily stored in India (Supabase's Mumbai or Singapore region — confirm with the Supabase project settings). Some data (video bytes) is processed by Cloudflare Stream, which has a global CDN. By using Streamly, you consent to the transfer of your data to these jurisdictions.

If you are a user in the EU/UK, this section needs expansion (Standard Contractual Clauses, GDPR Article 44 et seq.). v0.7.0 is not formally launched in the EU; revisit when v0.7.1 expands.

## 16. What this outline does NOT cover (v0.7.1+ scope)

- **Cookie policy** — not applicable for the native app, but if a web admin dashboard is added in v0.7.1, a separate cookie disclosure is needed.
- **Children's verifiable parental consent** — DPDPA doesn't currently require it, but if a kids' mode is added, COPPA-style verifiable consent is required.
- **AI/ML training opt-out** — currently moot (no AI features), but if v0.7.1+ adds AI captioning, recommendations, or content moderation, the policy needs an explicit opt-out clause per DPDPA Section 8.
- **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)** — required under DPDPA for certain processing activities. Not required for v0.7.0's scope; revisit if the data set expands significantly.
